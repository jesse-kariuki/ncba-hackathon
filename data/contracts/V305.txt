MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT between NCBA Bank Kenya PLC and Jamhuri Bulk SMS Ltd.
1. Fees: KES 140,000 per month, payable within 30 days of a valid KRA eTIMS invoice.
2. Term: this agreement expires on 2027-06-30.
3. Price review: fees increase by 5% on each anniversary.
5. Exit: NCBA may terminate on 90 days notice; the Supplier shall support transition.
6. Audit: NCBA and the Central Bank of Kenya may audit the Supplier's controls.
