MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT between NCBA Bank Kenya PLC and Teams Rooms - Rift Valley Cloud Ltd.
1. Fees: KES 370,000 per month, payable within 30 days of a valid KRA eTIMS invoice.
2. Term: this agreement renews automatically on 2026-12-20 unless either party gives 21 days written notice.
3. Price review: fees increase by 4% on each anniversary.
4. Data protection: the Supplier shall comply with the Data Protection Act 2019 and process personal data only on NCBA's written instructions.
5. Exit: NCBA may terminate on 90 days notice; the Supplier shall support transition.
6. Audit: NCBA and the Central Bank of Kenya may audit the Supplier's controls.
